## 2026-06-19 - Accessible Image Overlay Actions\n**Learning:** Icon-only overlay buttons inside `opacity-0 hover:opacity-100` containers are invisible to keyboard users when focused, and lack context for screen readers.\n**Action:** Always pair `group-hover:opacity-100` with `group-focus-within:opacity-100` on overlays containing interactive elements. Add `aria-label` to icon-only buttons, and provide visible focus indicators.
