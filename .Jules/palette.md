## 2024-11-08 - Accessible Download Button
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons revealed only on hover hide functionality from keyboard users and screen readers, making them inaccessible.
**Action:** Add `focus-within` styles to reveal controls on keyboard navigation, and ensure icon-only buttons always have an `aria-label` and keyboard focus indicators.
